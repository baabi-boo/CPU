named record and replace every `not_recorded` value with actual observations or

## Artifact index

No artifacts exist for this template. In a real record, list every log, trace,
report, generated design, or measurement with its stable location, byte size,
SHA-256, source/run identity, retention/access policy, and redactions. A summary
alone must not hide missing raw evidence. Do not include secrets or restricted IP.

- Limitations and unresolved blockers: not_recorded.

- Applicable prerequisite gates and evidence references: not_recorded.
- Gate decision: **not reviewed**; no `go`, conditional approval, or gate pass.
- Follow-up issue, evidence invalidation, or rollback action: not_recorded.
